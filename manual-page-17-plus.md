<h1 align="center">Plus</h1>

Plus represents traditional addition when dyadic.

Dyadic `+` adds its arguments. Monadic `+` is Signum: less than zero yields minus one; greater than zero yields one; zero yields zero.

Examples:

- #0: 1 + ∅ ⍠ ∅ ⋄
