<h1 align="center">Quotient</h1>

Quotient represents real division when dyadic.

Dyadic `÷` divides its left argument by its right, yielding a real number. Division by zero yields infinity — except zero divided by zero, which yields Nil. Monadic `÷` yields the reciprocal: a back-rank scalar operator.
